Privacy policy

Effective 10 October 2026.

Olympus is a knowledge engine that runs on your Mac and answers questions about your own mail, files and notes, including questions you ask in ChatGPT. It is published by Open Coordination Unlimited, Inc. ("OCU", "we", "us"), a United States company, which is responsible for the personal data described here. This policy explains what data Olympus processes, where, why, who receives it, how long it is kept, and how you control it. When it changes, the date above changes.

In short: Olympus stores your source collection and the index it builds on your Mac; we do not maintain a hosted copy. When you ask a question in ChatGPT, ChatGPT receives the question it sent, plus answers and excerpts built from items Olympus has labeled Personal, with citations. Items labeled Private are excluded from what ChatGPT's model receives; answers from them are shown to you in an Olympus panel inside ChatGPT, encrypted between that panel and your Mac. Requests between ChatGPT and your Mac pass through our relay, which forwards them without intentionally storing their content. If you set up anonymous questions, a question you choose to ask goes to zkAPI and an AI model provider without your identity attached. Automatic labels can be wrong.

Privacy policy in brief

The same facts as the detailed sections below, under the usual headings.

Personal data we collect

OCU holds little personal data, because Olympus runs on your Mac. Olympus does not require an OCU user account, and OCU does not currently take payments for Olympus. What we hold:

  • Relay registry: for each install, an install identifier derived from the install's public key, the public key, and timestamps.
  • Relay event logs: event types (for example "session ready"), times, fixed diagnostic fields and a shortened one-way hash of the install identifier.
  • In memory only: IP addresses and request counters used to limit abuse, and which installs are online.
  • In transit: your questions, answers, evidence excerpts and the other data described in What ChatGPT receives, while the relay forwards them between ChatGPT and your Mac.
  • Support correspondence: your email address, name if you give it, and anything else you send us.

On your Mac, under your control and not collected by us, Olympus processes the content of the sources you connect, the index it builds, sign-in tokens for your accounts, your settings and local logs (see Categories of data).

How we use it

To answer your questions from your own sources when you ask, to connect ChatGPT to your Mac once you approve it there, to keep the relay secure and working, and to answer support requests. We do not sell personal data, use it for advertising, build profiles, or train models on it.

Who receives it

  • OpenAI (ChatGPT) receives your questions and what Olympus returns: answers and excerpts from Personal items, citations, item names, status, and the data shown in Olympus panels (see What ChatGPT receives).
  • Google, Dropbox and other sources you connect are read directly by your Mac with the access you grant.
  • Services you choose to set up: Venice, for private answers; zkAPI and the AI model provider it reaches, for anonymous questions (see Anonymous questions); any other provider you configure.
  • Our service providers: Hetzner Online GmbH (relay and website hosting, Germany) and our email service provider (support email).
  • Authorities, where the law requires, limited to information we hold.

How long we keep it

  • Relay registry: active registrations expire after about 90 days without a connection, or sooner if you ask (see Retention for what the relay keeps after that).
  • Relay event logs: 14 days.
  • In-memory data on the relay: only while needed; the relay's code does not write it to disk.
  • Support email: until your request is resolved, then deleted unless the law requires longer.
  • Account and billing data: none; we do not hold any.
  • On your Mac: until you delete it. Finished pending answers about 15 minutes; private answer jobs 10 minutes and private question jobs 30 minutes, in memory.

Your controls and rights

You choose which sources Olympus reads, and you can disconnect ChatGPT, export Olympus's data from your Mac, or delete it, at any time (see Your controls). To get a copy of the personal data we hold, correct it, or delete it, email support@olympusplugin.ai. We respond without undue delay, normally within 30 days and within any shorter deadline the law sets. We may ask for information to confirm the request is yours. Rights you have under the law where you live are not affected.

The three labels

Olympus labels every item, one by one, as Personal, Private or Secret. Item names and titles are labeled separately from their contents, and are Personal by default, so the name of a Private item may be visible in ChatGPT even though its contents are not.

  • Personal items may be used to answer questions in ChatGPT.
  • Private items (for example health, money, legal and identity matters, and whatever you tell Olympus is private for you) are excluded from the answers and excerpts ChatGPT's model receives. They are answered on your Mac, by the private model built into Olympus or by Venice if you choose to set it up, and the answer is shown to you in the private answer panel (see The private answer panel).
  • Secret items (for example passwords, recovery codes and private keys) are excluded from answer retrieval and model inputs. Olympus keeps a record that a Secret item exists.

Labeling is automatic and can be wrong. Olympus is designed to choose the more protective label when it is unsure.

Categories of data

Processed on your Mac

  • Content from sources you connect: mail, documents and files from accounts you connect, such as Gmail, Google Drive and Dropbox, and from other sources you set up on your Mac, such as X bookmarks, Readwise, Telegram or WhatsApp. This can include any information those items contain, including text, images and audio.
  • Derived data: the search index, text extracted from documents and recordings, vector representations made by the built-in models, labels and their reasons.
  • Credentials: the sign-in tokens that let Olympus read the accounts you connect, the keys and tokens that link your Mac to ChatGPT, and, if you set up anonymous questions, your zkAPI account keys.
  • Settings and logs: your Olympus settings and local log files. Olympus's logs are designed not to contain message content or tokens.

What ChatGPT (OpenAI) receives

Olympus returns data to ChatGPT in two ways: tool results that ChatGPT's model reads, and Olympus panels displayed inside ChatGPT. Panel-only data is not given to ChatGPT's model, but it passes through OpenAI's systems.

  • Questions and answers: the questions ChatGPT sends to Olympus on your behalf; answers and evidence excerpts built from Personal items; citations with source name, title, date and link where available; item names that are Personal, which can include the name of an item whose contents are Private; how many items were searched or not yet read; and temporary identifiers used to collect an answer that is still being prepared.
  • When Private items match: a fixed note to ChatGPT's model saying so, with no titles or content. For the private answer panel only: how many Private items matched, progress, a temporary job identifier and a public encryption key.
  • The Olympus dashboard and status: connection and source status, when sources last synced and what was found, indexing and download progress, item counts, names of files Olympus could not read and why, model choices and their status, recent activity times, troubleshooting status and diagnostic references. When you set up remote access, it also shows the configured SSH user name or host name and local service port, to give connection instructions.
  • Choosing folders and mail (panel only): folder names, identifiers or paths, how folders are nested, sizes and file counts; mail label names and identifiers, mail categories and counts; suggested sender addresses with how many messages each sent; the sender addresses you mark as always Private or skip; your saved choices; and technical revision markers that keep these choices consistent. Locations you keep Secret are left out.
  • Connecting a source: the service you chose, a one-time sign-in link and when it expires.
  • Your privacy settings: your own description of what is private for you, and how many folders, labels or senders you marked as always Private; the panel also shows which ones.
  • Cited sources: when you open a cited source that is not on your Mac, its web address, which ChatGPT opens for you.
  • Anonymous questions asked in the conversation: the answer, the model that gave it, the exact question that was sent, the privacy level and preparation you chose, and whether network privacy was verified (see Anonymous questions).
  • The private question panel: ChatGPT's model learns only that the panel opened, or why it could not. The panel receives a temporary job identifier, a public encryption key and your saved level; the question you type and its answer are encrypted between the panel and your Mac.
  • Errors: fixed error messages and, for some errors, an opaque diagnostic reference.

The private answer panel

When Private items match a question, an Olympus panel inside ChatGPT shows you an answer from them, marked "Not sent to ChatGPT". Your Mac writes the answer and encrypts it with a key that only that panel holds; the panel fetches it from your Mac through the relay, not through ChatGPT's conversation. ChatGPT's model does not receive it, and the relay forwards only encrypted data. The panel's code is served inside ChatGPT, so this protection also depends on your Mac, the panel's code as ChatGPT delivers it, and the key exchange.

The panel keeps its encryption keys, job identifiers, creation times and question-panel progress in the browser storage ChatGPT gives it. Entries older than 24 hours are rejected or removed when the panel next uses or cleans that storage; unused entries can remain until a later cleanup or until you clear ChatGPT's site data. Answer text is not written to that storage.

What OpenAI does with your ChatGPT conversations, including Olympus results and panels, is governed by OpenAI's terms and privacy policy and your ChatGPT settings, not by this policy.

Anonymous questions (optional)

If you set it up on your Mac, Olympus can ask a frontier AI model a question for you through zkAPI, using your own zkAPI account. It does this only when you ask for an anonymous or private question.

  • What leaves your Mac: the prepared question and the technical information needed to route and run it, such as the chosen model. Olympus does not attach source documents or conversation history. In Strict mode, your own model on your Mac first rewrites the question into general questions to remove identifying details; in Standard mode it goes as you wrote it or lightly prepared, and can keep identifying details. Olympus checks it before it leaves.
  • Who receives it: the zkAPI service and the AI model provider it reaches, which can read the question. zkAPI is designed to separate who pays from what is asked, so your questions are not attached to your account. Olympus sends the question through Tor unless you turn Tor off, but cannot currently verify network anonymity on macOS. The content of a question can itself identify you.
  • Where you ask it: a question you write in the ChatGPT conversation, and its answer, are seen by OpenAI and pass through our relay readable, like other Olympus results. A question you type in the private question panel, and its answer, are encrypted between the panel and your Mac.
  • What stays on your Mac: your zkAPI account keys, and a usage record (counts, costs and technical checks for each session; not your questions or answers).
  • Your zkAPI account is your own, and is set up and managed in Olympus on your Mac, outside ChatGPT. OCU does not sell zkAPI access or receive, hold or have custody of your zkAPI funds. Adding to the account is an Ethereum transaction, which is public on that network; zkAPI is designed so that your questions cannot be linked to it.

zkAPI and the model provider keep what they receive according to their own terms. OCU does not receive anonymous questions you type in the private question panel, or their answers, in readable form.

What the relay processes

ChatGPT reaches your Mac through the relay at mcp.olympusplugin.ai. Your Mac keeps an encrypted, outgoing connection open to the relay, and the relay forwards ChatGPT's requests along it.

  • In transit: the relay ends the encrypted connection from ChatGPT (TLS), so request and response contents pass through its memory while being forwarded. Its code does not intentionally store them.
  • Logs: the relay's operational logs record event types, times, fixed diagnostic fields and a shortened one-way hash of your install's identifier. Its code does not intentionally log request or response content, tokens or credentials, query strings, or caller IP addresses. The web server in front of the relay is configured with access logging off.
  • Registry: for each install, an install identifier derived from its public key, the public key, and timestamps. It holds no name, email, IP address, account or token.
  • In memory only: IP addresses and counters used to limit abuse, and which installs are online.
  • Tokens: the relay does not issue or check the tokens ChatGPT uses. Your Mac does.
  • Private answers and private questions: the relay forwards the panel's requests and your Mac's replies encrypted, without the key to read them, and does not log them or their identifiers.

Our website

Our website, olympusplugin.ai, uses no cookies, analytics or trackers, loads nothing from other sites, and is configured with access logging off.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, build profiles of you, or use your content to train models.

Where the EU or UK GDPR applies, we rely on: performance of our agreement with you, to run the relay and connect ChatGPT to your Mac; our legitimate interests in keeping the relay secure and working; and our legal obligations where they apply. Third parties and recipients

  • OpenAI provides ChatGPT, which you use to ask Olympus questions. It receives what is described in What ChatGPT receives.
  • Google and Dropbox (and other sources you choose) are reached directly from your Mac through their official APIs, with access you grant using your own accounts. Olympus asks for read-only access: for Google, read-only access to Gmail, Google Drive and, if you choose, Google Calendar; for Dropbox, read access to file content, file metadata and sharing information. You can withdraw that access in your account settings with those providers at any time. Olympus's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google is used only to answer your own questions, is not transferred to others except as described in this policy (for example to ChatGPT when you ask), is not used for advertising, and is not read by people at OCU.
  • Venice (optional). If you choose to connect a Venice account, Olympus may send the text of Private items relevant to a question to Venice to produce an answer, or to build search vectors. Venice's handling, including how long it keeps what it receives, is governed by Venice's own terms and privacy policy.
  • zkAPI and AI model providers (optional). If you set up anonymous questions, the prepared question and the technical information needed to run it go to the zkAPI service and the AI model provider it reaches (see Anonymous questions). Their handling is governed by their own terms.
  • Other providers you configure yourself, for example an embedding provider, receive what that setting sends.
  • Hosting: the relay and website run on a server rented from Hetzner Online GmbH in Germany. Email to support is handled by our email service provider, which processes messages on our behalf.
  • Legal requests: we do not maintain a hosted copy of your source collection. We may disclose information we hold where the law requires it.

Retention

  • On your Mac: until you delete it. Uninstalling Olympus stops it but keeps its data until you delete that too (see Your controls).
  • Answers in progress: a finished pending answer stays available on your Mac for about 15 minutes, so ChatGPT can collect it.
  • Private panels: private answer jobs expire 10 minutes after they are created and private question jobs after 30 minutes. They are held in memory on your Mac and removed by periodic cleanup. An answer already shown can stay visible while its panel is open. For the panel's browser storage, see The private answer panel.
  • Relay registry: active registrations expire after about 90 days without a connection. Earlier registry entries stay on disk until the relay restarts and compacts its records. The relay keeps up to 20,000 expired install identifiers, with no fixed time limit, to recognise returning installs, and keeps the identifiers of revoked installs until they are restored, so that a blocked install cannot reconnect.
  • Relay event logs: kept for 14 days, then deleted.
  • Support email: kept as long as needed to resolve your request, then deleted unless the law requires us to keep it longer.
  • Anonymous questions: Olympus keeps a usage record on your Mac (counts, costs and session checks, not questions or answers) until you delete Olympus's data.
  • Account and billing data: Olympus does not require an OCU account and OCU does not currently take payments for Olympus, so we hold none.
  • OpenAI, zkAPI, Venice and the providers you connect keep data according to their own policies.

Your controls

  • Choose sources: connect and disconnect sources in the Olympus dashboard.
  • Disconnect ChatGPT: remove Olympus in ChatGPT, or revoke ChatGPT's access on your Mac from the Olympus dashboard or with olympus connections list and olympus connections revoke <id>. After revoking, ChatGPT cannot reach your Mac until you approve it again on the Mac.
  • Privacy settings: tell Olympus in your own words what is private for you, and mark folders, labels or senders as always Private, in the Olympus panel.
  • Anonymous questions: off unless you set them up; turn them off at any time in the Olympus dashboard on your Mac.
  • Revoke account access: remove Olympus's access in your Google or Dropbox account settings.
  • Stop Olympus: olympus engine uninstall stops Olympus and removes its login item. It keeps your settings and data.
  • Export your data: olympus data export --output <folder> exports supported indexed data and sanitized settings. Credentials and some operational state are left out; the export's manifest lists what was included and skipped.
  • Delete your data: olympus data delete --all deletes the index and data Olympus stored on your Mac (add --dry-run first to see what it will remove). This cannot be undone.
  • Relay registry: ask us to remove your install's registry entry by emailing support.

Sensitive information

Connected sources can contain sensitive information, such as health or financial records, government identifiers, or passwords. Olympus processes the sources you select on your Mac and uses automatic labels to limit what is disclosed; labels can be wrong. Olympus does not ask you for this information. Do not submit payment card data, protected health information, government identifiers, passwords, API keys or authentication codes through the ChatGPT plugin, including its panels.

Security

Connections are encrypted in transit. ChatGPT must be approved on your Mac before it can reach it, and only ChatGPT's published client is accepted. The relay cannot issue tokens or approve new connections. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach affecting you, we will tell you as the law requires.

Children

You must be at least 13 to use Olympus, the same minimum age as ChatGPT. If you are under 18, you need permission from a parent or guardian. Olympus is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly process personal data from them. If you believe a child under 13 has used Olympus, contact us and we will delete any data we hold about them.

International transfers

OCU is based in the United States. Our relay is hosted in Germany. OpenAI and the providers you choose may process information in other countries under their own policies.
