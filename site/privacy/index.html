Privacy policy

Effective 10 October 2026.

Olympus is a knowledge engine that runs on your Mac and answers questions about your own mail, files and notes, including questions you ask in ChatGPT. It is published by Open Coordination Unlimited, Inc. ("OCU", "we", "us"), a United States company, which is responsible for the personal data described here. This policy explains what data Olympus processes, where, why, who receives it, how long it is kept, and how you control it. When it changes, the date above changes.

In short: your mail, files, notes and the index Olympus builds from them stay on your Mac. We do not have a copy. When you ask a question in ChatGPT, ChatGPT receives the question it sent, plus excerpts built only from items Olympus has tiered Personal, with citations. It never receives the contents of Private or Secret items. Answers from Private items are shown only to you, in an Olympus panel inside ChatGPT, sealed so that neither ChatGPT's conversation nor our relay can read them. The Olympus relay that connects ChatGPT to your Mac passes requests through without storing or logging their content. If you set up anonymous questions, only a question you choose to ask goes out, to zkAPI and an AI model provider, without identifying you.

Privacy policy in brief

The same facts as the detailed sections below, under the usual headings.

Personal data we collect

OCU collects very little, because Olympus runs on your Mac. We do not run user accounts or take payments, so we hold no names, passwords, account records or billing data. What we do hold:

  • For each install, a random install identifier, its public key, and timestamps (the relay registry).
  • Short relay event logs (for example "session ready"), with a time and a one-way hash of the install identifier. No content, tokens or IP addresses.
  • IP addresses and request counters, in memory only, to limit abuse.
  • Your questions, answers and evidence excerpts, only in memory while the relay forwards them between ChatGPT and your Mac. They are not stored or logged.
  • Anything you send us when you email support, such as your email address and message.

On your Mac, under your control and not collected by us, Olympus processes the content of the sources you connect, the index it builds, sign-in tokens for your accounts, your settings and local logs (see Categories of data).

How we use it

To answer your questions from your own sources when you ask, to connect ChatGPT to your Mac once you approve it there, to keep the relay secure and working, and to answer support requests. We do not sell personal data, use it for advertising, build profiles, or train models on it.

Who receives it

  • OpenAI (ChatGPT) receives your questions and what Olympus returns: answers and excerpts from Personal items, citations, item names, and status (see What ChatGPT receives).
  • Google, Dropbox and other sources you connect are read directly by your Mac with the access you grant.
  • Services you choose to set up: Venice, for private answers; zkAPI and the AI model provider it reaches, for anonymous questions (see Anonymous questions); any other provider you configure.
  • Our service providers: Hetzner Online GmbH (relay and website hosting, Germany) and our email service provider (support email).
  • Authorities, only where the law requires, and only the little we hold.

How long we keep it

  • Relay registry entry: until the install is removed; removed automatically after 90 days without a connection, or sooner if you ask.
  • Relay event logs: 14 days.
  • In-memory data on the relay (requests in transit, IP addresses, counters): only while needed, never written to disk.
  • Support email: until your request is resolved, then deleted unless the law requires longer.
  • Account and billing data: none; we do not hold any.
  • On your Mac: until you delete it. Pending answers about 15 minutes; private answers at most 10 minutes, in memory.

Your controls and rights

You choose which sources Olympus reads, and you can disconnect ChatGPT, export everything Olympus holds on your Mac, or delete it, at any time (see Your controls). To get a copy of the personal data we hold, correct it, or delete it, email support@olympusplugin.ai. We answer within 30 days, and sooner where the law requires; we may need to confirm the request is yours. Rights you have under the law where you live are not affected.

The three tiers

Olympus labels every item, one by one, as Personal, Private or Secret. Item names and titles are labeled separately from their contents, and are Personal by default, so the name of a Private item may be visible in ChatGPT even though its contents are not.

  • Personal items may be used to answer questions in ChatGPT.
  • Private items (for example health, money, legal and identity matters, and whatever you tell Olympus is private for you) are never sent to ChatGPT, not even as summaries. They are answered on your Mac, by the private model built into Olympus or by Venice if you choose to set it up (see Third parties), and the answer is shown only to you in the private answer panel (see The private answer panel).
  • Secret items (for example passwords, recovery codes and private keys) are removed from the index and are never given to any model. Olympus keeps only a record that a Secret item exists.

Labeling is automatic and can be wrong. Olympus is designed to err towards the more protective tier when it is unsure.

Categories of data

Processed only on your Mac

  • Content from sources you connect: mail, documents and files from accounts you connect, such as Gmail, Google Drive and Dropbox, and from other sources you set up on your Mac, such as X bookmarks, Readwise, Telegram or WhatsApp. This can include any information those items contain.
  • Derived data: the search index, text extracted from documents, vector representations made by the built-in model, tier labels and their reasons.
  • Credentials: the sign-in tokens that let Olympus read the accounts you connect, and the keys and tokens that link your Mac to ChatGPT.
  • Settings and logs: your Olympus settings and local log files. Olympus's logs are designed not to contain message content or tokens.

What ChatGPT (OpenAI) receives

  • The questions ChatGPT sends to Olympus on your behalf.
  • Answers, and excerpts of evidence, built only from Personal items.
  • Citations for those items: source name, and title, date and link where available.
  • Item names that are Personal, which can include the name of an item whose contents are Private.
  • When some items matching a question are Private: a fixed note to ChatGPT's model saying so, with no titles or content. Alongside it, for the private answer panel only (not ChatGPT's model), how many Private items matched and a one-time identifier for the private answer.
  • Status shown in the Olympus dashboard: which kinds of sources are connected, indexing progress, and anything that needs your attention. It never includes Private or Secret items.
  • When you choose folders or mail in the Olympus panel: the names of the folders you browse, your mail labels, and the sender addresses you mark as always Private or skip, shown to you in the panel but not given to ChatGPT's model. Locations you keep Secret are left out.
  • When you open a cited source that is not on your Mac: its web address, which ChatGPT opens for you.
  • Your privacy settings, when ChatGPT asks for them: your own description of what is private for you, and how many folders, labels or senders you marked as always Private.
  • When you ask an anonymous question in the conversation: the answer, the model that gave it, and the exact question that was sent (see Anonymous questions). A question you type in the private question panel instead never reaches ChatGPT; ChatGPT learns only that the panel opened.

The private answer panel

When Private items match a question, an Olympus panel inside ChatGPT shows you an answer from them, marked "Not sent to ChatGPT". Your Mac writes the answer and seals it with a key that only that panel holds; the panel fetches it from your Mac through the relay, not through ChatGPT's conversation. ChatGPT's model never receives it, and the relay sees only sealed data. The panel's code is served inside ChatGPT, so this protection also relies on that code as ChatGPT delivers it. A private answer is kept on your Mac, in memory, for at most 10 minutes.

What OpenAI does with your ChatGPT conversations, including this material, is governed by OpenAI's terms and privacy policy and your ChatGPT settings, not by this policy.

Anonymous questions (optional)

If you set it up on your Mac, Olympus can ask a frontier AI model a question for you anonymously, through zkAPI, using your own zkAPI account. It does this only when you ask for an anonymous or private question.

  • What leaves your Mac: only the question, with no documents and no conversation history. Depending on the level you choose, your own model on your Mac first rewrites it into general questions (Strict), or it goes as you wrote it, lightly prepared (Standard). Olympus checks it before it leaves.
  • Who receives it: the zkAPI service and the AI model provider it reaches. The provider can read the question but is not told who sent it: zkAPI uses zero-knowledge proofs so your use cannot be tied to your account, and the question is sent through Tor unless you turn Tor off. An unusual question can still hint at who you are.
  • Where you ask it: a question you write in the ChatGPT conversation is also seen by OpenAI, like anything else you write there. A question you type in the private question panel is sealed to your Mac and its answer is shown only in that panel; neither ChatGPT's conversation nor our relay can read them.
  • What stays on your Mac: your zkAPI account and its keys, and a usage record (counts, costs and technical checks for each session; not your questions or answers).
  • Your zkAPI account is set up and managed in Olympus on your Mac, outside ChatGPT. Adding to it is an Ethereum transaction, which is public on that network; zkAPI is designed so that your questions cannot be linked to it.

zkAPI and the model provider keep what they receive according to their own terms. OCU does not receive your anonymous questions or answers.

What the relay processes

ChatGPT reaches your Mac through the relay at mcp.olympusplugin.ai. Your Mac keeps an encrypted, outgoing connection open to the relay, and the relay forwards ChatGPT's requests along it.

  • In transit: the relay ends the encrypted connection from ChatGPT (TLS), so request and response contents pass through its memory while being forwarded. It does not store them.
  • Not logged: request and response bodies, tokens and credentials, query strings, and caller IP addresses are not logged. The web server in front of the relay keeps no access log.
  • Logged: short operational events (for example "session ready" or "request refused") with a time and a short one-way hash of your install's identifier.
  • Stored: a registry entry for each install: a random install identifier, the install's public key, and timestamps. It holds no name, email, IP address, account or token.
  • In memory only: IP addresses and counters used to limit abuse, and which installs are online.
  • The relay does not create, check or store the tokens ChatGPT uses. Your Mac does.
  • Private answers: the relay forwards the panel's request and your Mac's sealed reply. It cannot read the answer, and it logs neither the answer nor its identifier.
  • Private questions: a question typed in the private question panel, and its answer, pass through the relay sealed in the same way. The relay cannot read them and does not log them.

When you are not using Olympus with ChatGPT

Our website, olympusplugin.ai, uses no cookies, analytics or trackers, loads nothing from other sites, and keeps no access log.

Why we process data

  • To answer your questions from your own sources, as you ask.
  • To connect ChatGPT to your Mac, and only to your Mac, once you approve it there.
  • To keep the relay secure and available: rate limits, abuse prevention, and fixing faults.
  • To respond when you contact support.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, build profiles of you, or use your content to train models.

Where the EU or UK GDPR applies, we rely on: performance of our agreement with you, to run the relay and connect ChatGPT to your Mac; our legitimate interests in keeping the relay secure and working; and our legal obligations where they apply. The content on your Mac is processed by Olympus on your own computer under your control.

Third parties and recipients

  • OpenAI provides ChatGPT, which you use to ask Olympus questions. It receives what is described in What ChatGPT receives.
  • Google and Dropbox (and other sources you choose) are reached directly from your Mac through their official APIs, with access you grant using your own accounts. Olympus asks for read-only access: for Google, read-only access to Gmail, Google Drive and, if you choose, Google Calendar; for Dropbox, read access to file content, file metadata and sharing information. You can withdraw that access in your account settings with those providers at any time. Olympus's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google is used only to answer your own questions, is not transferred to others except as described in this policy (for example to ChatGPT when you ask), is not used for advertising, and is not read by people at OCU.
  • Venice (optional). If you choose to connect a Venice account, Olympus may send the text of Private items relevant to a question to Venice to produce an answer, or to build search vectors. It does this only if you set it up. Venice's handling, including how long it keeps what it receives, is governed by Venice's own terms and privacy policy.
  • zkAPI and AI model providers (optional). If you set up anonymous questions, the question you choose to ask, and nothing else, goes to the zkAPI service and the AI model provider it reaches, without identifying you (see Anonymous questions). Their handling is governed by their own terms.
  • Other providers you configure yourself, for example an embedding provider, receive only what that setting sends.
  • Hosting: the relay and website run on a server rented from Hetzner Online GmbH in Germany. Email to support is handled by our email service provider, which processes messages on our behalf.
  • Legal requests: we may disclose the limited data we hold (see relay) where the law requires it. We cannot disclose your content: we do not have it.

Retention

  • On your Mac: until you delete it. Uninstalling Olympus stops it but keeps its data until you delete that too (see Your controls).
  • Answers in progress: a pending answer is held on your Mac for about 15 minutes after it is ready, so ChatGPT can collect it.
  • Relay registry: until the install is removed. An install that has not connected for 90 days is removed automatically.
  • Relay event logs: kept for 14 days, then deleted. They contain no content (see relay).
  • Support email: kept as long as needed to resolve your request, then deleted unless the law requires us to keep it longer.
  • Anonymous questions: Olympus keeps a usage record on your Mac (counts, costs and session checks, not questions or answers) until you delete Olympus's data.
  • Account and billing data: we do not run accounts or take payments, so we hold none.
  • OpenAI, zkAPI, Venice and the providers you connect keep data according to their own policies.

Your controls

  • Choose sources: connect and disconnect sources in the Olympus dashboard.
  • Disconnect ChatGPT: remove Olympus in ChatGPT, or revoke ChatGPT's access on your Mac from the Olympus dashboard or with olympus connections list and olympus connections revoke <id>. After revoking, ChatGPT cannot reach your Mac until you approve it again on the Mac.
  • Privacy settings: tell Olympus in your own words what is private for you, and mark folders, labels or senders as always Private, in the Olympus panel.
  • Anonymous questions: off unless you set them up; turn them off at any time in the Olympus dashboard on your Mac.
  • Revoke account access: remove Olympus's access in your Google or Dropbox account settings.
  • Stop Olympus: olympus engine uninstall stops Olympus and removes its login item. It keeps your settings and data.
  • Export your data: olympus data export --output <folder>.
  • Delete your data: olympus data delete --all deletes the index and data Olympus stored on your Mac (add --dry-run first to see what it will remove). This cannot be undone.
  • Relay registry: ask us to remove your install's registry entry by emailing support; otherwise it is removed after 90 days without a connection.

Sensitive information

Your own sources may contain sensitive information, such as health or financial records, government identifiers, or passwords. Olympus does not ask for this information and OCU does not collect it. Olympus is designed to label it Private or Secret so it is not sent to ChatGPT. Please do not paste such information into ChatGPT yourself.

Security

Connections are encrypted in transit. ChatGPT must be approved on your Mac before it can reach it, and only ChatGPT's published client is accepted. The relay cannot issue tokens or approve new connections. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach affecting you, we will tell you as the law requires.

Children

You must be at least 13 to use Olympus, the same minimum age as ChatGPT. If you are under 18, you need permission from a parent or guardian. Olympus is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly process personal data from them. If you believe a child under 13 has used Olympus, contact us and we will delete any data we hold about them.

International transfers

OCU is based in the United States, and the relay runs in Germany. Requests from ChatGPT pass through the relay from wherever OpenAI processes them. Where we transfer personal data out of the EU or UK, we rely on an appropriate safeguard, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or an adequacy decision.
