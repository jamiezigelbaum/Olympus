Fix it on your Mac

A few things can only be set up on your Mac, because they need a key or a pairing that never goes through ChatGPT. When the Olympus dashboard in ChatGPT says to do something on your Mac, this page says what.

Open Olympus on your Mac

Open Terminal on your Mac and run:

olympus dashboard

It opens the Olympus dashboard in your Mac's browser. The link works once, for 15 minutes; run the command again for a new one. If Terminal says Olympus is not running, see ChatGPT says your Mac is offline.

Connect a source ChatGPT can't connect

ChatGPT connects Gmail, Google Drive and Dropbox. These sources are connected on your Mac instead:

  • X bookmarks use an X developer app of your own.
  • Readwise uses your Readwise access token, pasted on your Mac.
  • Telegram and WhatsApp pair with your Mac.

Open Olympus on your Mac, find the source under Sources and choose Connect; the page walks you through it. Then choose Check again in the dashboard in ChatGPT.

Reconnect a source

When X bookmarks or Readwise stop syncing because their sign-in or token expired, open Olympus on your Mac, find the source under Sources and reconnect it (for Readwise, paste a new token). Gmail, Google Drive and Dropbox reconnect from ChatGPT with Reconnect.

Answers have stopped working

This means the answer model you set up on your Mac cannot be reached. Open Olympus on your Mac and look under Models:

  • If you use Venice, check that its key is still valid and your account has credit, and paste a new key if needed.
  • If you use a local model, make sure the model server it names is running on your Mac.

Then choose Check again in the dashboard in ChatGPT. Personal answers in ChatGPT keep working meanwhile.

Search has stopped working

Your Mac uses a custom search (embedding) model that cannot be reached. Make sure the model server it names under Models is running on your Mac, then choose Check again. Changing the search model means indexing everything again, so Olympus only does that on your Mac, when you ask.

Change models

Models and their keys are set up on your Mac, never in ChatGPT: open Olympus on your Mac and use Models. The built-in models need no setup.

Still stuck?

Contact support and include the output of olympus engine status. Check it first: it contains file paths on your Mac, but no message content.
