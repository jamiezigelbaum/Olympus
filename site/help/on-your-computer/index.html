Fix it on your computer

A few things can only be set up on your computer, because they need a key, a pairing or your private things, none of which go through ChatGPT or any other AI app. When Olympus says to do something on your computer, this page says what and where.

What is only on your computer

These live only in Olympus on your computer, under On this computer. No AI app can show them or change them.

  • Private answers: questions your agents asked that need your private things, waiting for you to answer them on this computer, and the Anonymous answers switch. Open Private answers.
  • Keys: your Venice, Readwise and X keys.
  • Agents: remote access, and the agents connected to Olympus, each with Revoke.
  • Build a connector: for a source Olympus does not have yet.

To reach them, open Olympus on your computer, then choose Open next to the one you want under On this computer.

Open Olympus on your computer

On the computer running Olympus, go to olympusplugin.ai/open/dashboard and choose Open Olympus; if your browser asks whether to open Olympus, choose Open. From the Olympus dashboard in ChatGPT, Connect and Fix this on your computer do the same for you. If nothing opens (on a phone, or with an older Olympus), open Terminal on your computer and run:

olympus dashboard

It opens the Olympus dashboard in your computer's browser. The link works once, for 15 minutes; run the command again for a new one. If Terminal says Olympus is not running, see ChatGPT says your computer is offline.

On a Linux computer without a screen

If Olympus runs on an Omarchy computer you reach over SSH, only your own account there can open Olympus or approve an AI app. From the computer you sit at, open a secure tunnel to it (olympus doctor on that computer prints the exact path for your user; it looks like this):

ssh -N -o ExitOnForwardFailure=yes -L 127.0.0.1:8010:/run/user/1000/olympus/worker.sock you@your-computer

Then, on that computer, run olympus dashboard --no-open and open the link it prints on the computer you sit at.

Approving ChatGPT, Claude or Grok works the same way: the approval page opens through the tunnel.

Connect a source ChatGPT can't connect

ChatGPT connects Gmail, Google Drive and Dropbox. These sources are connected on your computer instead:

  • X bookmarks use an X developer app of your own.
  • Readwise uses your Readwise access token, pasted on your computer.
  • Telegram and WhatsApp pair with your computer.

Open Olympus on your computer, find the source under Sources and choose Connect; the page walks you through it. Then choose Check again in the dashboard in ChatGPT.

Gmail and Google Drive, when Google sign-in is full. Google limits how many people can sign in through Olympus's own Google app. When the dashboard says Google sign-in is full for the moment, connect Gmail and Google Drive with a Google app of your own instead: open Olympus on your computer, choose Set up next to Gmail or Google Drive and follow the steps. When Google asks, publish your app to production; an app left in testing stops signing in after 7 days.

Reconnect a source

When X bookmarks or Readwise stop syncing because their sign-in or token expired, open Olympus on your computer, find the source under Sources and reconnect it (for Readwise, paste a new token). Gmail, Google Drive and Dropbox reconnect from ChatGPT with Reconnect.

Answer private questions, or turn on anonymous answers

Both are on the Private answers page on your computer: open Private answers, or open Olympus on your computer and choose Open next to Private answers under On this computer. Not under Models: that is only for changing models.

  • Waiting questions. When ChatGPT asks something only your private things can answer, or another agent's private answer could not be given, the question waits here, and Needs you on your computer says so. Choose Answer to answer it on this computer with the model built into Olympus, or Dismiss. You can also ask your own question in Ask privately. Nothing on this page leaves your computer.
  • Anonymous answers are under Anonymous answers on the same page. They are on Mac only for now, and off until you turn them on there; your agent can then send a question anonymously, through zkAPI and Tor, using your own zkAPI account.

In Olympus 1.0.0-rc.6 and earlier there is no Private answers page yet: Anonymous answers has its own row under On this computer.

Answers have stopped working

This means the answer model you set up on your computer cannot be reached. Open Olympus on your computer and look under Models:

  • If you use Venice, check that your account has credit, and that its key is still valid: keys are on Keys, under On this computer. Paste a new key there if needed.
  • If you use a local model, make sure the model server it names is running on your computer.

Then choose Check again in the dashboard in ChatGPT. Personal answers in ChatGPT keep working meanwhile.

Search has stopped working

Your computer uses a custom search (embedding) model that cannot be reached. Make sure the model server it names under Models is running on your computer, then choose Check again. Changing the search model means indexing everything again, so Olympus only does that on your computer, when you ask.

Change models

Models are set up on your computer, never in ChatGPT: open Models on your computer, or open Olympus on your computer and use Models. Their keys are on Keys, under On this computer. The built-in models need no setup. Anonymous answers are not here: they are on Private answers.

Still stuck?

Contact support and include the output of olympus engine status. Check it first: it contains file paths on your computer, but no message content.
