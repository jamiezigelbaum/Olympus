ChatGPT says your computer is offline

Olympus answers from your computer, so your computer has to be awake, online and running Olympus. When it is not, ChatGPT shows "Your computer is offline" and answers pause. Nothing is lost; answers resume when the computer is back.

1. Wake your computer

  • Open the lid or press a key, and log in.
  • Check the computer is connected to the internet.
  • If your computer often sleeps while you are away, you can keep it awake while plugged in: in System Settings, open Battery (or Energy) and turn on the option that prevents automatic sleeping when the display is off.

2. Check Olympus is running

Open Terminal on your computer and run:

olympus engine status
  • "ok": true means the engine is running and answering on your computer.
  • Under relay, the state shows whether your computer is linked to the Olympus relay.
  • If missing lists anything, follow what it says. For example, "The engine agent is installed but not loaded" means running olympus engine install again.

If the engine is installed but stuck, restart it:

olympus engine restart

To see recent log lines (they contain no message content or tokens):

olympus engine logs --lines 50

3. Try again in ChatGPT

Ask your question again, or open the Olympus dashboard in the ChatGPT sidebar. It shows whether your computer is connected and when it was last seen.

Still offline?

  • A VPN, firewall or network filter on the computer may block the outgoing connection to mcp.olympusplugin.ai. Olympus needs only an outgoing HTTPS connection; it opens no ports on your computer.
  • If you have just uninstalled and reinstalled Olympus, you may need to approve ChatGPT again on your computer.
  • Otherwise, contact support and include the output of olympus engine status. Check it first: it contains file paths on your computer, but no message content.
