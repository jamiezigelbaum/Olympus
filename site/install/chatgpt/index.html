Connect ChatGPT

ChatGPT reaches Olympus on your Mac through the Olympus relay. You approve it once, on the Mac, with one click: no code to copy and no account to create.

Olympus is on its way to ChatGPT's app directory. Until it is listed, you add it in Developer mode, which ChatGPT offers on Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise and Edu plans.

Before you start

  • Olympus installed on your Mac. One line in Terminal.
  • Be at that Mac. The approval opens in its browser.

Connect

  1. Turn on Developer mode. In ChatGPT, open Settings, then Apps, and turn on Developer mode under Advanced settings.
  2. Create the connector. Create a connector, name it Olympus, choose OAuth, and paste this address as the URL: 
    https://mcp.olympusplugin.ai/mcp
    Then choose Create.
  3. Approve on your Mac. A page opens in your Mac's browser, Approve ChatGPT on your Mac, and moves on by itself to Connect ChatGPT to Olympus? Choose Connect. That click is how Olympus knows the Mac is yours.
  4. Connect your sources. Ask ChatGPT Show my Olympus dashboard. Choose Connect next to Dropbox, Gmail or Google Drive, pick folders, then Save and start.
  5. Ask. Ask about your own things in plain words. Anything Private is answered on your Mac and shown in a panel marked Not sent to ChatGPT.

Good to know

  • If the approval page says Olympus did not answer on this computer, it opened somewhere other than the Mac where Olympus runs. Start again from ChatGPT on that Mac.
  • Your Mac needs to be awake and online for ChatGPT to reach Olympus. It opens no ports; it only makes an outgoing connection. If ChatGPT says your computer is offline.
  • The relay passes requests between ChatGPT and your Mac. It stores and logs no questions, answers or tokens.
  • To disconnect, delete the Olympus connector in ChatGPT. On the Mac, the dashboard's Agents page (under On this computer) lists connected apps, each with Revoke.
