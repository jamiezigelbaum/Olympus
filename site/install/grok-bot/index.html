Connect Grok Bot

Grok Bot reaches Olympus on your Mac through the Olympus relay. You approve it once, on the Mac, with one click: no code to copy and no account to create.

Before you start

  • Olympus installed on your Mac. One line in Terminal.
  • Be at that Mac. The approval opens in its browser.

Connect

  1. Add the server. In Grok Bot, add a custom MCP server named Olympus with this address: 
    https://mcp.olympusplugin.ai/mcp
  2. Approve on your Mac. A page opens in your Mac's browser asking to connect Grok Bot or Cursor to Olympus: Grok Bot signs in the way Cursor's cloud agents do, so the page names both. Choose Connect.
  3. Ask. Ask about your own things in plain words. Grok Bot searches what is Public and Personal. A question that needs your Private things is not answered in Grok Bot: it waits on your Mac, and Needs you says so. Open Private answers on the Mac to answer it there; the answer stays on the Mac.

Good to know

  • If the approval page says Olympus did not answer on this computer, it opened somewhere other than the Mac where Olympus runs. Start again from Grok Bot on that Mac.
  • Your Mac needs to be awake and online for Grok Bot to reach Olympus. It opens no ports; it only makes an outgoing connection. If Grok Bot says your computer is offline.
  • The relay passes requests between Grok Bot and your Mac. It stores and logs no questions, answers or tokens.
  • To disconnect, remove the Olympus server in Grok Bot. On the Mac, the dashboard's Agents page (under On this computer) lists connected apps, each with Revoke.
