Connect Grok

Grok on grok.com reaches Olympus on your Mac through the Olympus relay. You approve it once, on the Mac, with one click: no code to copy and no account to create.

Before you start

  • Olympus installed on your Mac. One line in Terminal.
  • Be at that Mac. The approval opens in its browser, so start on grok.com on the Mac.

Connect

  1. Add the connector. Go to grok.com/connectors, choose New Connector, then Custom, and paste this address as the MCP server URL: 
    https://mcp.olympusplugin.ai/mcp
  2. Sign in. Continue to sign in.
  3. Approve on your Mac. A page opens in your Mac's browser asking to connect Grok to Olympus. Choose Connect. That click is how Olympus knows the Mac is yours.
  4. Ask. Ask about your own things in plain words. Grok searches what is Public and Personal. A question that needs your Private things is not answered in Grok: it waits on your Mac, and Needs you says so. Open Private answers on the Mac to answer it there; the answer stays on the Mac.

Good to know

  • If the approval page says Olympus did not answer on this computer, it opened somewhere other than the Mac where Olympus runs. Start again from Grok on that Mac.
  • Your Mac needs to be awake and online for Grok to reach Olympus. It opens no ports; it only makes an outgoing connection. If Grok says your computer is offline.
  • The relay passes requests between Grok and your Mac. It stores and logs no questions, answers or tokens.
  • To disconnect, remove the Olympus connector at grok.com/connectors. On the Mac, the dashboard's Agents page (under On this computer) lists connected apps, each with Revoke.
