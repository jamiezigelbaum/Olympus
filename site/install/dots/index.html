Connect Dots

Dots, OpenAI's always-on agent, uses the apps connected to your ChatGPT account. Connect Olympus to ChatGPT once, and Dots can ask it too.

Connect

  1. Install Olympus on your Mac. One line in Terminal.
  2. Connect ChatGPT. Follow Connect ChatGPT, with the same ChatGPT account Dots uses.
  3. Ask Dots. Ask about something only your own files would know.

Good to know

  • Dots asks while your Mac is awake and online, the same as ChatGPT.
  • Private things stay on your Mac. Dots gets only the answers your rules allow.
