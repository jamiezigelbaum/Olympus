Install Olympus on your Mac

Olympus runs on your Mac and answers your questions in ChatGPT. Installing it takes one line in Terminal.

Before you start

  • A Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later). Macs with an Intel processor are not supported.
  • macOS 13 Ventura or later.
  • The ChatGPT desktop app.

Install

Open Terminal (in Applications, then Utilities), paste this line, and press Return:

curl -fsSL https://olympusplugin.ai/install.sh | sh

It takes a minute or two. When it says Olympus is installed and running, go back to ChatGPT. Running the same line again later updates Olympus, or repairs it if it stopped. You can read the installer before you run it.

Then connect ChatGPT

  1. Add Olympus in ChatGPT. In the ChatGPT desktop app, add the Olympus plugin and ask: Connect Olympus.
  2. Approve ChatGPT on your Mac. An Olympus approval page opens in your Mac's browser. Click Approve. That click is how Olympus knows the Mac is yours: there is no code to copy and no account to create.
  3. Connect your sources. Open the Olympus dashboard in the ChatGPT sidebar and connect Dropbox, Gmail or Google Drive, signing in with the accounts you already have. Other sources the dashboard lists, such as X bookmarks or Readwise, are connected on your computer. Indexing starts on its own.

What the installer does

  • Installs Olympus for your macOS user only. It needs no administrator password and changes nothing outside your home folder.
  • Downloads two things over a secure connection, and checks each against a published checksum before using it: Bun, the runtime Olympus runs on (about 24 MB), and Olympus itself (about 5 MB). If either does not match, it stops and changes nothing.
  • Starts Olympus in the background as a per-user login item (a LaunchAgent), so it is running whenever you are logged in, and checks that it really started.
  • Adds the olympus command, and a line to your shell profile so Terminal finds it.
  • If an update does not start, puts the previous version back.

After that, Olympus downloads its built-in models in the background:

  • A model used to search your items on the Mac (about 225 MB).
  • A private answer model that stays on the Mac: about 1.3 GB with 8 GB of memory, about 2.7 GB with 16 GB or more. It downloads only while the disk has room for it plus 2 GB to spare.

Nothing from your files leaves the Mac during install.

Check that it is running

In a new Terminal window:

olympus engine status

Look for "ok": true. If something is missing, the missing list says what to do next. See also what to do when ChatGPT says your computer is offline.

Where Olympus keeps things

FolderWhat is in it
~/Library/Application Support/OlympusThe Olympus app, the previous version, and the Bun runtime
~/.local/bin/olympusThe olympus command
~/Library/LaunchAgents/ai.olympusplugin.engine.plistThe login item that keeps Olympus running
~/.olympusOlympus settings and your privacy choices
~/.config/olympusSettings for Olympus's background worker
~/.local/share/openclaw/olympusYour index, the built-in models, and this Mac's connection keys
~/Library/Logs/OlympusLogs, including install.log

Uninstall

In Terminal:

curl -fsSL https://olympusplugin.ai/uninstall.sh | sh

This stops Olympus, removes its login item, and deletes the app, the runtime, the olympus command and the line the installer added to your shell profile. It keeps your data and settings, and lists where they are. To only stop Olympus and remove its login item, keeping the app, run olympus engine uninstall instead.

To delete your data too, do this before uninstalling. It cannot be undone:

olympus engine stop
olympus data delete --all

Add --dry-run to the second line first to see what it will remove. To disconnect ChatGPT, remove Olympus in ChatGPT. See Your controls in the privacy policy for more.
