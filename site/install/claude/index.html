Connect Claude

Claude reaches Olympus on your Mac through the Olympus relay, on the web, in the desktop app and on your phone. You approve it once, on the Mac, with one click: no code to copy and no account to create.

Before you start

  • Olympus installed on your Mac. One line in Terminal.
  • Be at that Mac. The approval opens in its browser, so start in Claude on the Mac.

Connect

  1. Add the connector. In Claude, open Customize, then Connectors. Choose +, then Add custom connector, name it Olympus, and paste this address as the URL: 
    https://mcp.olympusplugin.ai/mcp
    Keep the options Claude fills in, then choose Add.
  2. Connect. Choose Connect on the Olympus connector.
  3. Approve on your Mac. A page opens in your Mac's browser asking to connect Claude to Olympus. Choose Connect. That click is how Olympus knows the Mac is yours. After that, Olympus works in Claude on your phone too.
  4. Ask. Ask about your own things in plain words. Claude searches what is Public and Personal. A question that needs your Private things is not answered in Claude: it waits on your Mac, and Needs you says so. Open Private answers on the Mac to answer it there; the answer stays on the Mac.

Good to know

  • If the approval page says Olympus did not answer on this computer, it opened somewhere other than the Mac where Olympus runs. Start again from Claude on that Mac.
  • Your Mac needs to be awake and online for Claude to reach Olympus. It opens no ports; it only makes an outgoing connection. If Claude says your computer is offline.
  • The relay passes requests between Claude and your Mac. It stores and logs no questions, answers or tokens.
  • To disconnect, remove the Olympus connector in Claude. On the Mac, the dashboard's Agents page (under On this computer) lists connected apps, each with Revoke.
