Introducing Olympus.

A private second brain that lives on your computer and works with your AI.

Add Olympus to your AI

Your privacy and the best AI.

Not one or the other: both.

Your whole life, in one place.

Gmail, Google Drive, Dropbox, Telegram, WhatsApp, X bookmarks and Readwise, indexed on your Mac. More coming, or build your own.

Every item, judged on its own.

Each email, file and message is marked Personal, Private or Secret. Secrets like passwords never go anywhere.

Your machine, your memory.

When an app asks for full access to everything, Olympus blocks it.

Your Mac answers first.

Small models built into Olympus run on your Mac: Qwen3.5 4B for private answers, EmbeddingGemma 2 for search and photos, Qwen3-ASR for voice notes and Apple Vision for scans. Private answers are not sent to the cloud.

Venice.ai hides what you ask.

Private models, and nothing stored.

zkAPI hides who’s asking.

Paid anonymously and sent over Tor. No account, no card, no name: just proof the question is paid for. Open source, from the Ethereum Foundation’s zkAPI.

How an anonymous question leaves your Mac.

Your Mac rewrites the question without your name, your numbers or your accounts, pays for it anonymously and sends it over three Tor hops, with a fresh key for each question.
