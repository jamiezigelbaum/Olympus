Should I use a private AI service or run AI locally?

Run AI locally when the data must never leave your computer and a smaller model is good enough. Use a private cloud service when you need stronger answers and trust its no-logging promise. For your own mail and files, a mix often works best: keep the data local and send only what is safe to the best model.

The trade-offs

Local modelPrivate cloud service
Where your text goesNowhere: it stays on your computerTo the service, which says it keeps no logs
Answer qualityBehind the best cloud modelsClose to frontier models
HardwareNeeds memory and a recent chipAny device
ExamplesOllama, LM Studio, JanProton Lumo, Venice, Duck.ai

Neither protects you if you paste the wrong document into the wrong place. And a private chat service does not know your own mail and files unless you paste them in.

Using both with Olympus

Olympus keeps your mail, files and messages on your computer and uses a small model there for Private items, sized to run on a computer with 8 GB of memory. Personal items can go to ChatGPT, Claude or another assistant you connect. For harder private questions you can turn on Venice's private models, and optionally ask anonymously through zkAPI over Tor. Both are off by default. See the privacy policy for what each choice sends.
