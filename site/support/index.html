Support

We are glad to help with installing Olympus, connecting sources, and answers that look wrong.

Contact

  • Email: support@olympusplugin.ai
  • Bug reports and feature requests: GitHub issues. Issues are public, so do not post personal information, message content or log output you have not checked.

Before you write

  • ChatGPT says your Mac is offline: see this help page.
  • Installing: see Install.
  • What ChatGPT can and cannot see: see the privacy policy.

What to include

  • Your macOS version and the Olympus version (run olympus --version in Terminal).
  • What you asked and what happened, in your own words.
  • The output of olympus engine status, if relevant.

We will never ask for your passwords, API keys, or the contents of your mail or files. Support cannot see your data: it stays on your Mac.
