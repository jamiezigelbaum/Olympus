Support

Olympus is free, open source and made in the open. There is no help desk: questions, bugs and ideas go on GitHub, where they are read and worked through as time allows.

Start here

  • Installing: Install.
  • Something not working on your computer: Help.
  • Your AI says your computer is offline: this help page.
  • What your AI can and cannot see: the privacy policy.

Report a bug or ask for a feature

Open a GitHub issue. Issues are public, so leave out personal information, message content and any log output you have not checked. Include:

  • Your computer (a Mac or Omarchy) and the Olympus version (run olympus --version in a terminal).
  • Which AI app you used, what you asked, and what happened.
  • The output of olympus engine status, if relevant.

Nobody will ever ask for your passwords, API keys, or the contents of your mail or files. Nobody else can see your data: it stays on your computer.

Security problems

Report them privately, not in a public issue: email support@olympusplugin.ai with "Security" in the subject.

Privacy requests

Your data is on your computer, not with us; to delete it, see Your controls. We hold no account, name or email address for you. The relay keeps only an identifier for each install, so your computer can reconnect, and short-lived logs. To have your install's record removed, email support@olympusplugin.ai. This address is only for security and privacy; for anything else, please use GitHub.
